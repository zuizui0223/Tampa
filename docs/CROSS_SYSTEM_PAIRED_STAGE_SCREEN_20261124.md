## Actual source calculation and scope
Kendall & Nicholson (2025), Ecology Letters 28:e70036, DOI 10.1111/ele.70036, data DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.26004538.v1. Retrieved the actual public Git blob 39a82d966afcdde670603add1c881ace8791ffe9 from liamkendall/bumble-bat, data/DvN_LiteratureDataClean_2024-09-13.csv. Contents API returned empty content for this large file; Git blob retrieval succeeded. CSV parsed with quoted-field and multiline handling, 1652 rows, 140 distinct study IDs; all exclude=n. This is the deposited extraction table, NOT the paper's final analyzed sample of 135 studies/1094 effect sizes. The difference is retained, not reconciled. All new numerical screening performed in-session directly on the source blob; Python reproducer 165 added, CI pending.

Counts: fruit set 913, seed set 606, fruit mass 60, seed mass 34, pollen deposition 35, other 4 treatment means. There are 441 day and 441 night rows, plus other treatments. Group day/night records by study_ID, species, cultivar, country, locality, Site_period, coordinates, start/end years/months, additional_treatment. Require exactly one row per day/night arm within each endpoint and identical deposited unit labels. This is a candidate metadata match, not a verified shared biological experiment, flower, or cohort. No averaging over ambiguous duplicate arms. Missing context metadata may hide subgroups; coordinates/months can distinguish nominally identical sites without establishing actual treatment identity.

298 candidate metadata contexts; 127 contain more than one matched endpoint, from 64 study IDs. 113 contain fruit and seed set, of which 112 have finite nonnegative arm means (53 study IDs). Of those 112, 88 same-direction, 21 opposite-direction, 3 with a tie. Fifteen study IDs have at least one opposite-direction candidate. Equal-study average opposite fraction .207884; not population prevalence, no inferential CI. Distinct site/season contexts within one paper are not independent studies. 
Posthoc stricter label/positive-means gate: explicit fruit proportion/fruit-per-flower label (including deposited converted-count labels), seed-per-fruit/pod label, fruit means <=1 and all four means >0. 40 contexts in 18 IDs, 7 opposite, 33 same, 5 IDs with opposite; equal-study average .192460, leave-one-study-out .144958–.203782. Six opposites have absolute log ratios >= log(1.1) for both endpoints. More conservative sensitivity additionally drops converted-count labels (#) and both-arm seed means below 1, giving 16 contexts /13 IDs, 2 opposite (Dar and Lemaitre). This is a sensitivity, not a valid exclusion criterion for biological seed counts or confirmed conditional denominators. Some deposited seed/fruit labels appear to describe normalized quantities. Report direction only, not an across-study magnitude, sign-significance prevalence, or causal mechanism. Full candidate row IDs and original values saved in results/diel_pollination_stage_screen_20261124.json. Do not use products of arm means as observed seeds per flower without original denominator/cohort confirmation.

## Original-paper checks of two candidates
Dar et al. 2006, DOI 10.1093/aob/mcj045, Table 2 and text. Marginatocereus marginatus, diurnal fruit proportion .028 vs nocturnal .0833, conditional seeds per fruit 159 vs94.5. Source paper has only 1 diurnal and 2 nocturnal fruits, and text/table disagree on 36/37 diurnal flowers. Both fruit treatments have same significance letter. Do not claim a robust difference or compensation mechanism. Original paper already interprets complementary diurnal/nocturnal guilds. 
Lemaitre et al. 2014, Generalized pollination system: Are floral traits adapted to different pollinators?, Table 3 from accessible author copy. Echinopsis chiloensis: nocturnal exclusion (=day access) fruit proportion .48, seeds/fruit 764.8; diurnal exclusion (=night access) .20, 1599.0. Fruit-set contrast significant, seed-set comparison nonsignificant (Tukey p=.309). The authors themselves reported fruit x seed products 367 vs320; near balance is not a new calculation or evidence of an exact compensation mechanism. 
Pollen deposition labels occur in only 8 study IDs, and only 2 also contain seed set (Martinell_2010 and Elam_1988), yielding 5 candidate contexts; not a large independent pollen-to-seed meta-analysis. Carthew pollen metric is pollen-tube presence, not deposited grains, and Tan uses pollinaria. Martinell 2010 Plant Ecology paper text (DOI 10.1007/s11258-010-9785-y) gives 43.7 seeds/fruit at night, versus deposited 47.3 (same n43/SE3.2); flag discrepancy, do not silently correct extracted file. Elam 1988 has multiple color-morph/site combinations; original-paper subgroup verification required before effect pairing. No mediation estimates from extraction table.
