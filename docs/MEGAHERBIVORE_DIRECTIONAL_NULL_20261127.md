# Distance-matched directions and between-component delivery (2026-11-27 JST)

## Purpose and source
Continuation of 207, same source archive and animals; not a new independent study. Gredzens et al. 2014 (10.1371/journal.pone.0098944), Carter et al. 2022 (10.26274/2CR2-JK51); source archive c17dcaa395b9dabc6ab5b61ce918e1abc68fe87b528a2bd12c0e01dd2a97920f verified locally and all inner hashes checked. The Torres subset is 1,875 cleaned fixes. Day-level data and five-day-pair minimum as in 207. GSHHS intermediate resolution from installed Basemap supplies a coarse endpoint land mask (exact hashes in result). No raw coordinates reproduced in this commit. 
For each possible observed fix pair at date lags 6,7,10,13, preserve observed origin and WGS84 geodesic separation, evaluate uniformly spaced bearings (36 main; 72 sensitivity). Null is endpoint-only conditional on bbox, or bbox plus water endpoints by coarse GSHHS, not navigable path, current flow or actual passive dispersal. Retain matched observed pairs satisfying the same relevant endpoint restriction (water version also restricts origin), origin mapped and >=1 valid bearing. Equal date-pair weight, then normalize among selected pairs per animal, then equal animal. Within each map, actual and null have identical origins and distances. 
Use dissolved union polygons, 159 features with at least one survey date in 2008–12, alternative +/-300m and all-date union. Disconnected polygon components are geometric units, not population or validated meadow IDs. Components (near, eroded, buffered, all) 124/94/75/279; largest fraction of union area .754/.782/.935/.709, respectively, including complete features returned by bbox query. Changing buffer alters topology and eligible origins, not a simple measure of biological patch boundaries. All endpoint percentages are relative to observed mapped starts under that map and distance group.

Dugong >=10 km: mapped excess +8.07pp, positive in 3/5; leave-one-animal range -0.21 to +16.18pp. Other-component excess -8.14pp, positive in only 1/5; leave-one-animal range -10.29 to -4.54pp. Thus conditional long-distance endpoint retention is not the same as cross-component connection; the geometric null does not support a robust positive enrichment of other-component arrival in this panel. Not evidence of a causal effect, nor population-level significance. Relative deficit in other-component arrival persists with erosion (10.43 vs16.44), buffer (0 vs .56; near saturation by giant component) and all dates (3.49 vs10.21). All-map turtle long-distance any-map overlap much higher than near-map, so no robust species ranking. At 5-degree bearings, dugong near-map long results 53.36 vs45.21, 10.86 vs18.97; turtle 43.83 vs41.27, 0 vs14.40. 
Important contrast: apparent 'other habitat' deficit could arise from within-range movements, coastline geometry, sampling and historical map incompleteness; source habitat not necessarily ingestion, endpoint not excretion. Null directions are not advective transport. Observed within-day fixes can be correlated; no inferential p-values. No observed genetic connection or recruitment. Do not combine with pooled Tol viability or infer sea-otter effect.
