## Functional coverage boundary
Exact three locally studied species cover 16.664% of displayed vegetative fauna and 16.716% of flowering fauna by ratio of site-equal count sums. This includes the nondetected-effect Caprella, and only A. valida has direct local reproductive consumption evidence. Thus ~83% lack even this selected local vegetative-effect mapping; unknown cannot be scored neutral. Quantitative behavior/max size/feeding/predation trait coverage is only 1.980% V and 2.772% F; conditional means in JSON are matched-only indices, not estimates of total community body size, locomotion, grazing flux or cascading effect. Undefined summaries serialize as null. No extrapolation to all juveniles, all amphipods, or Caribbean Thalassia visitors. 2012 Thalassia visitor diet labels are not direct pollen-transfer effectiveness; 2016 mixed-fauna pollen tube experiment lacks taxon-specific seed output. Net pollination benefit minus seed losses not estimable.

## New tissue-stage accounting from Reynolds primary Table1
Units mg consumed per amphipod per day; rounded means, choice alternatives and collection dates differ between experiments, covariance not available. Late stage 4 spathes vs early stage 0–1 consumed 3x (0.3/.1) and 140x (.7/.005) in autumn Expts1/2; vs vegetative young leaves 4.286x and 7x. In Expt1 loose seeds on sediment .0001 vs inferred attached mature fruit loss .09, nominal 900-fold difference; near-zero rounded denominator and different tissue measures (attached fruit includes pericarp), not an estimated hazard ratio or proof of stage-specific individual mortality. In Expt2 inferred mature fruit loss .2. Control vs treatment difference in remaining fruits, sediment seed counts similar; paper attributes about 30% of stage 4 mass loss to fruits. Bicoastal trial SF Bay animals consumed CA vs VA leaves .3/.03 and stage4 spathes .5/.002 mg/day; ratios 10 and 250, descriptive only, different development and morphology, no general range-wide genotype effect. The original authors already report greater consumption of mature spathes, and show plant origin and habitat effects.

## Scenario only: concentration in minority flowering shoots
Use site-equal observed per-shoot count means and hypothetical common proportion f of flowering shoots; fraction of animals on flowering = fF/(fF+(1-f)V). f=.1 yields 23.23% total fauna and 25.22% A. valida; f=.2 yields 40.51% and 43.14%. Majority thresholds based on pooled means ~26.86% and 24.78%. Not observed shoot proportions, density estimate or total population response. Earlier Carr study explicitly notes areal counts mostly occur on vegetative shoots. Need actual matched flowering fraction, surface area, floral stage and functional activity per animal.

## Integration
Novel question is not whether reproductive shoots shelter animals (already experimentally tested), but whether local cooccurrence of functional taxa and reproductive morphology predicts net successful seed production and demographic renewal, accounting for predator control and uncertainty in taxa's functional roles. Directly test predator identity x reproductive-stage morphology x grazer access with tagged fruit and seed outcomes, and an independent Thalassia fauna x flow experiment for animal pollination. No actual net pollination-herbivory tradeoff estimated, no otter causal result, no pooled meta-analysis. Main/Paper A/TNC unchanged. Registry added 4 distinct targeted records, multistage eligibility added one.
