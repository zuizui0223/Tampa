## Gräfnings et al. 2023 DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.14405; data 10.34894/FKSY2M
18 plots, 3 blocks, 4 random 1 m2 squares per plot-year, 144 observations, 2020 July and 2021 August. Source methods specify different random squares, so do not pair replicate IDs across years. All plot-year totals >0. Conditional on total S in four measured squares, homogeneous independent equal-intensity benchmark multinomial(S; .25,.25,.25,.25). Descriptive dispersion D = sum((n_i-S/4)^2/(S/4))/3, expected 1 under benchmark. Mean D over 18 plot-years 1.2383 (2020) vs 1.9381 (2021), conditional 50,000 simulation p for excess .10874 vs .000020 (resolution 1/50001, not population-level p). Different sampling designs/habitat, possible overlapping or dependent quadrats, no explicit locations; conditional model diagnostic not formal randomization test. Paired plot mean year differences by block -.0307,+.7139,+1.4161; block mean +.69975, exact sign flip p=.5 under symmetry. Cannot establish general increase through generations. 2021 excluding 400 m2 high-density treatment drops D to 1.2539 (conditional p .1040), omitting two most dispersed plots to 1.2295 (.1179); leave-one-block-out D 1.6625–2.4732. Concentration of dispersion in a few 400 m2 high-dose plots matters. Zero quadrats 2021 4 observed vs 4.008 expected conditional on plot totals, 2020 0 vs .203. <=1 count 2021 11 vs 8.374 (p .0624 conditional). Therefore observed overdispersion does not establish excess zeros, let alone predation-induced local extinction.
