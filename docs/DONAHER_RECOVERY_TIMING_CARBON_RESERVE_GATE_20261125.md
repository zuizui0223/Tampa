## Important stop
Exp2 biomass sheet has 256 rows, all Year=2019, 86 duplicate context keys (plot/year/location/disturbance-month/species) beyond first; paper describes 2019 edge measurements and 2020 internal cores, so exact pre/post biomass-to-cover linkage not established. No silent year correction, no matching by row order, no carbohydrate reserves measured. Source data cannot establish that belowground reserve loss caused the late-season recovery difference. Disturbance timing can also change seed bank/recolonization opportunity and species phenology.
