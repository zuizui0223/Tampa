Jan 4 has only 40 sampled plots, omitted from ALL primary histories so observation opportunity is identical; nine complete visits from Nov5 2025 to Apr17 2026. All shoot counts nonnegative integers. No zeros filled for unsampled dates. Groups low 10–30, mid 40–70, high 80–100 follow original authors' visualization bins, not independently selected outcome groups. Ever-positive is any positive recorded count in the nine surveys, not actual emergence. Terminal-positive is recorded at Apr17. The 17-Dec landmark was used in prior analysis; early means any positive through that date. Reappearance is a positive after at least one intervening sampled zero following initial positivity; not biological recolonization. Counts are shoots, not distinct seedlings or genetic individuals.

## Three-part arithmetic identity
Let p=P(ever recorded positive), r=P(terminal positive|ever positive), m=mean terminal shoot count conditional on terminal positive. Mean terminal shoots per assigned plot=p*r*m. Because terminal positive implies ever positive, identity is exact. Symmetric average over all six factor replacement orders decomposes high minus low group mean into three additive components. This is not causal mediation; positivity selects different sets of plots. No independent row-level statistical inference, p-values, or meta-analytic weights.

## Relation to previous geometry/seed capture work
A local concentration increase cannot be directly converted into more new sexual recruits. This dataset fills the observed-plot appearance and terminal-state part of the chain, not the physical seed-capture transition. The prior Wadden experiment has strong initial density advantages attenuating by the second generation (different design/species/context), while this experiment shows an increasing within-season terminal-positive fraction conditional on any detection. The two outcomes cannot be pooled as a common survival probability. The independent study Statton et al. 2017, DOI 10.1038/s41598-017-13833-y, explicitly established life-stage-specific bottlenecks in Posidonia australis (early losses at sheltered shallow/intermediate sites and later complete losses at exposed sites); the paper is prior art, not new reanalysis. Thus novel target is not discovery that bottlenecks exist, but identifying when local seed concentration changes observation-history components and eventually net genotyped recruitment at the meadow scale. Need matched seed fate and independent randomized density/geometry/predator access treatments, separate detection calibration, longer follow-up. No pooled meta-analysis; no paper or main/TNC modifications.
