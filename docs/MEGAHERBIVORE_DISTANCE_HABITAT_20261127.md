## Estimand and design
For each animal, each observed day t with observed day t+d, d=1,6,7,10,13, form all origin/end position combinations uniformly within day pair; weight day pairs equally, then animals equally. No interpolation, no imputed time or missed day; actual elapsed time ambiguous by about one day. WGS84 geodesic net separation, not swimming path. Primary min 5 day pairs/animal, sensitivity min1/min10 and inclusion of land flags. Six/seven days align approximately to dugong retention; seven/thirteen bracket turtle retention, not a retention distribution. No population representativeness or independent pair-level inference. For mapped overlap, subset 159 polygons with any recorded survey year 2008–12; composite geometries can contain other periods, not year-specific habitat. Union in UTM54S; +/-300m erosion/buffer to assess sensitivity, full 316 polygon union additional historical map. Mapped footprint incomplete, outside not necessarily unsuitable, land or no grass; no species identity/phenology or seedling suitability. Only Torres Strait overlay, all conditioned ends within requested bbox. Map membership includes boundary. Origin-inside proxy means a sampled start fix coincides with mapped habitat, NOT ingestion. Calculate unconditional separation and conditional joint long separation + mapped end with same origin denominator. All proportions first calculated per animal, not ratios of pooled animal means. Compare long >=10km vs near <10km within animals with both possibilities; show thresholds 1/5/10/25 and lags sensitivity in full output; threshold 10 exploratory, not a biological breakpoint.

Full historical map gives .50482/.26254 and .24672/.14364 respectively. Same denominator for each within-map pair. Not counts of arriving seeds. Turtle estimates sensitive to temporal map coverage: near-map 7→13 days distance-only .38684→.51671, joint .05833→.03490; full map distance-only .24672→.34650, joint .14364→.15168, so direction of lag effect on joint outcome NOT robust to map. Do not assert longer retention decreases delivery for turtles.

Within five eligible Torres dugongs with mapped starts, compare endpoint overlap conditional on >=10km vs <10km, separately per animal then average. 6 days .56172 vs .76110, difference -19.94 pp, 5/5 negative. 7 days .53359 vs .75203, difference -21.84 pp, 5/5 negative; leave-one-animal-out difference -26.61 to -16.31pp. At 7 days map erosion -22.80pp, buffer -28.94pp (both 5/5 negative), all dates -22.15pp (4/5). 6-day alternative maps differences -18.46 to -23.72pp. This shows a recurrent *mapped habitat overlap penalty in these observed long-separation pairs*, not a mechanism or sampling-corrected movement effect. Pairwise associations can reflect map completeness, movement direction, individual range, location sampling, spatial autocorrelation. No significance claim from five individuals or dependent day pairs. No passive dispersal comparison (no water current/origin-matched counterfactual). Turtle long/near comparison only 3 eligible at 7d (one drives difference), 1–2 at 13d depending map. Do not equate distance with realized inter-meadow transfer; long displacements can remain inside large connected patches.

## Implications and boundary
The meaningful new quantity is joint opportunity: departure in mapped meadow, spatial separation and arrival overlapping mapped meadow; distinguish from potential distance and actual recruitment. To test seed dispersal efficiency, need contemporaneous species-specific recipient habitat, measured ingestion and excretion timing, viable seeds per event, individual tracks at suitable timestamps, and matched current-mediated alternative and actual establishment. These data are not available jointly; no inference about actual seed flux or relative vector efficacy, sea otter effect, or meta-analysis. No new Tol workbook, no main/manuscript edits.
