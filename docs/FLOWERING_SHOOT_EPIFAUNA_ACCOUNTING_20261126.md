Exact closure checked. Equal sites (within each site average available dates, then average sites) and equal pair summaries both reported. Geometric mean of F/V uses positive totals. Caprellids defined as juvenile caprellids, C. cf. drepanochir and C. californica; no assignment of pollination, granivory, origin or functional traits to juveniles.

## Interpretation and decisive next test
Reproductive accessibility requires more than animal abundance: available animals × contact with exposed reproductive structures × probability of a specified functional act given contact. Reproductive shoots can provide seasonal shelter/feeding habitat, which can raise local contact opportunity for beneficial and harmful taxa; observed standing counts alone do not measure contact or visitation, are confounded with shoot surface area, branching and epiphytes, and cannot establish the sign of reproductive outcome. A floral-associated animal may be a consumer, nursery resident, incidental visitor, or pollen carrier. A matched experiment with reproductive vs vegetative structure or inert structural analogues, comparable surface area/epiphytes, factorial animal access and directly tracked pollen delivery/seed damage/fate is needed. Distinguish Zostera results from Thalassia animal-pollination trials. No pooled meta-analysis, no causal otter effect, no evidence that greater per-shoot counts increase meadow-wide recruitment. Registry expanded 58→62 targeted records; multistage ledger 14→15 (new record labeled exposure proxy only). No main/Paper A/TNC changes.
