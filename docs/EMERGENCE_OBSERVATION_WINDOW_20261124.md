## Scenario boundary
For observed mesocosm emergence E(d) and hypothetical additional removable loss q(d), define R(d)=E(d)*(1-q(d)), only if independence and unchanged downstream emergence are assumed. To make depth d>1 cm preferable to 1 cm with q(d)=0, q(1) must exceed 1-E(d)/E(1). At 2 cm threshold 11.76%, 3 cm 40%, 4 cm 36.47%, 6 cm 78.82%, 8 cm 91.76%. These are necessary thresholds under perfect protection, not empirical predation risk estimates; if q(d)>0 the threshold increases. Infantes 2016 and UK 2024 differ in exposure, sediments and design; no cross-study multiplication as causal inference. This yields a testable prediction: predator-control benefits are likely stage/position dependent and protection alone may not rescue overburied seeds. No new finding that burial optimum exists, already known. Need independent randomized predator access x burial x bioturbator experiment with marked cohort and repeated fate measurements to test the full mechanism.
