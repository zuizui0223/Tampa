For nonnegative endpoint Y, mean(Y)=Pr(Y>0)*mean(Y|Y>0). The symmetric accounting of change uses (p1-p0)*(m1+m0)/2 and (m1-m0)*(p1+p0)/2. This is an exact arithmetic identity, not a causal mediation analysis. Conditioning on positivity selects different post-treatment units. Zero means observed zero at specified endpoint, not permanent population extinction.
