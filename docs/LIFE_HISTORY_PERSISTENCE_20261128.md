# Cross-region seagrass life-history persistence (2026-11-28 JST)
## Question
Is observed species persistence within the same site/year selectively associated with established persistent versus colonising/opportunistic life-history strategy? How often is focal taxon non-recording masked by another taxon of the same broad strategy?

Retain complete binary species fields consistent with whole-grass and richness fields. Exact coordinate/program/month/method/custodian, exact agreement within year; two previously examined Torres programs retain unknown month and specified 1/2-year lag. Other programs exact month and one-year lag. Gate by >=30 initial-positive pairs and >=10 coordinates; report all repeated-program counts. Tampa original pinned event/occurrence (existing verified source parser); full composition exact consensus within meter mark/year (stricter than old binary consensus), no new mechanism or forecasting fit.
- Pair-level fraction of initially recorded P taxa and other taxa still recorded on next survey, only cooccurrence at source. Equal cluster (Tampa transect, Australia coordinate) bootstrap; all initial cooccurrences primary, both-grass-positive subset sensitivity.
- Partition focal-taxon non-recordings into all grass unrecorded, another same-class taxon recorded, only other class recorded.
