## Fixed functional weights
Source artifact 14925937983, ZIP SHA256 5f0bdb9f65d277e3e0e52fd4d680893090bfb91a51ff9e742f750992c1b18117 verified; original workbook member hashes in analysis/170. Gallagher & Campbell 2020 DOI 10.1002/ajb2.1439 (Dryad 10.7280/D19X0D), 2021 DOI 10.3389/fevo.2021.641693 (Dryad 10.7280/D16D7Z). Original 2020 Table 1 already uses taxon-specific effectiveness x visit rate for weekly expected seed set; novelty here is fixed weights applied to the separate water x phenology experiment and held-out prediction comparisons, not the idea of functional weighting. Both experiments from 2016 and same research region, with zero overlapping plant IDs; not external study/site validation.

Calibration: 94 visited ramets, 42 Bombus and 52 others, 155/93 flowers respectively, seedCnt/noFlrs exactly equals seedPerFlr, noFlrs equals flrsVisited. Equal-ramet mean seeds/flower 1.269592709 vs .788324176; fix relative weights 1/.620926830. Equal-flower alternative 1.141935484 vs .741935484 (relative .649717514); conspecific pollen subset n=47, Bombus mean 1030.5, other 663.259259 (relative .643628587). Two calibration sites separately: relative other/Bombus .656038572 (RG) and .361134413 (SP), but composition and sampling weeks differ; no inference of site-specific intrinsic quality. Three-guild seed means Bombus 1.269593, Osmia 1.875 (8 ramets, RG only), fly .590747 (44); normalized weights 1/1.476852/.465304. Group mapping is a proxy, not species-specific transfer.

Target same 113 as analysis/169 (88 raw-agreement sensitivity); three-guild comparison separately uses 112 after additional count mismatch (plant 132011, classified non-Bombus subcounts exceed total by 3). Main weighted rate = author rate x [(Bombus count + w x non-Bombus count)/total observed visitor count]. Author rate measures flowers visited per available flower per observation hour, while group shares are counts of insect visitors; product is a proxy assuming comparable number of flowers visited per insect, not actual pollen or seed flux. Zero observed visits have zero rate; identical indicator in all visit models. Normalize to Bombus=1 to keep scale. No target outcomes used to calibrate w. Same number of continuous predictors for weighted and unweighted rate. Plant-level seed outcome bounded 0..4; ridge predictions not constrained. Training-fold standardization. Models identical to 169 except fixed weights and common linear week encoding in both array_linear and week_linear; original categorical-week array retained. Four week folds and 20 week-array folds, contemporaneous water, flower number and visit variables. No prospective prediction.

Primary fixed-weight model improves unweighted week prediction in 12/12 sensitivity combinations (2 panels x 2 water specifications x 3 penalties; reduction 2.63–3.66%); none outperforms context-only. The equal-flower, pollen, RG and SP two-class weights each improve unweighted week prediction in the same 12/12 but also never beat context. Primary fixed-weight model never improves unweighted array_categorical; common linear array model improves in 3/12, essentially negligible (<0.1%). With 112-plant three-guild panel, assigned-water alpha=1 week: unweighted 1.212740, three-guild seed weights 1.232207, seed-flower 1.245259, pollen 1.177414; both seed-weight variants worsen unweighted in all six penalty/water combinations. Do not claim taxonomic refinement improves prediction. Fixed grid other weight 0,.25,.5,.75,1,1.5,2 is hypothetical and not used to select a fitted parameter; even other=0 gives 1.08958 vs context 1.02154 in primary assigned alpha=1 week. No biological inference from extreme weight.

Primary fixed-weight vs unweighted week MSEs: W1 2.73655/3.08117, W2 .86203/.87460, W3 .58830/.58911, W4 1.28706/1.29434. All four folds improved relative to unweighted at alpha=1, but most absolute squared-error gain is from W1. Context wins in W1–3 and loses slightly W4. No independent fold inference, small same-region experiment, weights estimated with uncertainty and site/time confounding. Original sources already report differential effectiveness and context-dependent seed set. The bounded positive result is modest incremental performance relative to unweighted observed visitation in held-out weeks, not better than environment alone, not realized fitness or proof of a resource bottleneck. No cross-system generalization.
