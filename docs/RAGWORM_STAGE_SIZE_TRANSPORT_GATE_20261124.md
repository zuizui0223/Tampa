# Ragworm ontogenetic interaction: novelty correction and size-aware transport gate

2026-11-24. Published-aggregate extraction, source and design audit, and targeted acquisition submission; no new raw-data fit in this step.

## Direct prior evidence: do not repackage as novel
Zhu et al. (2016), Ecology 97:3278–3284, DOI 10.1002/ecy.1613, full text https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ecy.1613/full. In *Spartina anglica*, three-week feeding experiment: intact sterile seeds all retrieved intact, sprouts excised; independent four-week handling experiment redistributed ~25% of surface seeds into worm burrows, viability group test p=.89 (not equivalence). In a separate four-week feeding/growth study, 100% intact sterile seeds and 8.3% dehusked seeds recovered, all sprouts removed. The husk manipulation supports the accessibility mechanism. The original authors already explicitly proposed that worm mortality/predation before germination could change plant benefit; do not claim this hypothesis as original. No treatment directly randomizes the duration of worm access before versus after sprouting and measures net plant recruitment in this study. No independent seed-level raw data acquired; no uncertainty derived by pseudoreplicating seeds.

Delefosse & Kristensen (2012) DOI 10.1016/j.seares.2012.04.006 shows shallow relocation of intact eelgrass seeds by *Hediste diversicolor*; no net positive seed-to-recruitment effect measured. Kwakernaak et al. (2023) DOI 10.1016/j.jembe.2022.151853, full paper https://www.vliz.be/imisdocs/publications/49/385549.pdf: 40 mesocosms (eight combinations × five), 45 seeds per mesocosm, nominal initial biomass 0/2/8 g DW m−2 and food addition, separate small-worm 2 g condition. Published size comparison small 0.0029 vs large .0095 g DW/individual; 195±8 vs 9±9 seedlings m−2. Ratio 21.67, difference -186, 95.38% lower density in large-worm arm, reported F(1,17)=10.62, p=.0046. Approximately eight small versus three large worms per mesocosm; size and number co-vary under nominal fixed starting biomass. Not a direct size distribution or monotonic allometric function. At 8 g DW m−2, seedling establishment absent while intact seed recovery 52.7±2.3%, versus 38.2±4.2% at 0 g; +14.5 pp, ratio 1.38. Intact recovery is not viability or seed protection, and cannot establish which non-recovered seeds were consumed, when, or whether germination was inhibited. Raw data explicitly on request, not obtained. The text's 26-fold comparison does not match simple 364/9 arithmetic; avoid combining a time-averaged control with potentially different endpoint summaries. PDF text inspected; screenshot calls failed with cache miss, no digitization used.

## Independent three-organism replication opportunity
Hautekiet et al. (2026) Limnology and Oceanography DOI 10.1002/lno.70379, data DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15182547 (institutional catalogue https://www.vlaanderen.be/inbo/en-GB/datasets/data-van-competing-biotic-interactions-between-fibrous-algae-and-macrobenthos-shape-pioneer-vegetation-establishment-in-tidal-marshes/detail). In tidal marshes, *Vaucheria* algae mitigate *H. diversicolor* effects on *Aster tripolium* seedling survival. Data catalogue describes two field surveys, two transplant experiments, mesocosm and supplement data. This is an independent saltmarsh comparator, not evidence of sea otter or seagrass mechanism. Compare algae × worm assigned treatments with plant survival at a common terminal visit only if design verified; separate field habitat contrasts. No raw analysis yet. Archive may contain videos; cap each file at 40MB; preserve metadata if too large.

## Macroecological transport: planned diagnostics, not estimated risks
SIBES data DOI 10.25850/nioz/7b.b.ug, descriptor DOI 10.1038/s41597-025-04540-9. Published 51,851 sample records, 5,107 distinct stations, 2008–2021. Available abundance_m2 and afdm_m2 at sample × taxon resolution, not individual size distribution. Exact accepted taxon lookup and public files first; metadata and checksum preserved. No unprocessed samples set to zero. If processed status can be verified, separate true absence from missing biomass and keep zero abundance distinct from missing measurements. Restrict comparisons to observed positive *Hediste diversicolor* samples with nonmissing biomass; average individual AFDM = afdm_m2 / abundance_m2. Do not substitute genus group or infer body-size distribution from sample mean. Compare within-year and within-basin ranking under total biomass versus average individual mass and number; for repeated stations quantify stability of relative exposure across years, with equal station-year weight, grid vs additional stations sensitivity. No threshold transport: AFDM (ashed organic dry mass) differs from experimental DW; summer sampling differs from spring emergence. No plant recruitment data or verified suitability map; do not estimate unsuitable area or compare a new percentage directly to published 52.8%. A valid field risk model needs body-size distributions, calibrated DW conversion, seasonal overlap and plant endpoint. This is a source gate only.

A complementary Dryad dataset Zhu et al. 2015 (10.5061/dryad.23j62) has physical forcing × bioturbation burial data; active ragworm taxon is *Alitta virens*, not *Hediste*. Treat as physical pathway comparator. Public retrieval workflow submitted; no completion or acquisition claimed. Evidence register extended by five publications; targeted database, not a systematic review, no pooled estimate. Main and manuscript unchanged.
