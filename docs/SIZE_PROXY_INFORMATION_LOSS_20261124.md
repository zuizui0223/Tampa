# Aggregate size-proxy information loss: descriptive Glacier Bay audit

2026-11-24. The SIBES and Hautekiet sources remain unacquired at inspection. Run 45412432794 in progress, not a completed new analysis. To avoid importing an untested assumption into the future SIBES analysis, use existing verified USGS Glacier Bay 10.5066/P9LODH0Z size-frequency counts and site metadata from artifacts 14914948367 and 14915918501. No inference about Hediste, eelgrass performance, otter causality or restoration risk.

Randomly selected intertidal site-years only (92 matched events, 905 actually observed quadrats). Preserve counts and observed quadrat denominators; no zero imputation for missing samples. For each taxon and event, aggregate N=sum(count), mean observed linear size, density=N/nq, and size-cubed proxy C=sum(count*(size_mm/50)^3)/nq. C is **not measured biomass**. Compare to observed count density of organisms >=40,50,60 mm. Primary illustrative 50 mm, >=5 organisms per taxon-event; sensitivity all positive and >=10. Macoma and Mya are pooled genus groups as in source. All measurements restricted to sampled size >=14 mm. No biological threshold calibrated. Within-taxon descriptive Spearman correlations and upper-quartile overlap; define target >= empirical 75th percentile and strictly positive, include ties. Macoma has only eight positive events at 50 mm, so target is those eight, not a full quartile. Urchins only one positive, not suitable for evaluating recall at 50 mm. Repeated site-years and similar-event pairs are not independent; no p-values, no out-of-sample forecast. No claim these groups are functional equivalents.

For Saxidomus, density and cubic proxy recover all target events at all three thresholds, even >=10 individuals. For Mya, cubic proxy improves target recall at each threshold and count cutoff: 40/50/60 mm at >=5 counts, 7/11 to 9/11, 7/10 to 9/10, 5/12 to 6/12. At >=10 counts, 50 mm 6/9 to 7/9. Leukoma recall unchanged between proxies at all cutoffs, despite higher cubic rank correlation. Thus the information gain depends on size spectrum, taxon and endpoint; cannot assume adding mean size necessarily improves every classification. Comparison is same-sample reconstruction, not held-out prediction.

Near-matched density (ratio <=1.1) and mean length (within 3 mm) among eligible events: Saxidomus 1998 site 67 and 2011 site 43, both 13 organisms/10 quadrats, mean 61.85 vs 62.77 mm, observed >=50 mm fraction 11/13 vs 8/13 (84.62 vs 61.54%). Illustrative selected pair, not a representative effect; six eligible pairs, one exceeds 20 pp. Leukoma and Mya also each have one >=20 pp gap. Repeated observations and post hoc pair selection matter. Mean and total count do not determine upper tail. Jensen's inequality shows reconstructing sum(size^3) by N*(mean size)^3 underestimates actual third moment; median ratio 1.179 Leukoma, 1.163 Saxidomus, 1.255 Mya. This is arithmetic, not proof of ecosystem function. For hypothetical size-cubed mass, nonnegative mass gives upper bound f_large <= min(1,mean((size/50)^3)/(threshold/50)^3), not an exact prevalence. No transfer of shell lengths, sizes, coefficients or thresholds to ragworms.
