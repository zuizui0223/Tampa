## Accounting
Compare adjacent observed years within station, no gap filling. 1168/302 pairs. Primary descriptive short-lag panels: 1y lobster (1071, 155 stations), 2y cucumber (107, 55 stations). The 2y panel is selected for shorter comparable lag, NOT the modal cucumber lag; 3y has 125 pairs, mostly initial absence, and 4y/7y 26/44 pairs. All adjacent-gap summaries provided; no program comparison or pooled meta effect. Restrict to initial grass-positive. Average over pairs and within each pair equally over previously observed species. Three exhaustive outcomes: no grass recorded at revisit, grass recorded but focal prior taxon not recorded, focal taxon reobserved. This is an identity, not proof of excess mortality or a significance test.

## Prediction diagnostic
503 initial-positive 1y lobster pairs. Predict whether each focal taxon is recorded at next survey (including all-grass absence). Baseline separate taxon logistic ridge C=1 on standardized recorded depth and log1p(initial richness). Add focal initial presence as one feature. Scaling training only; taxa eligible if at least20 positives and20 negatives in training outcomes. Eight taxa in spatial folds. GroupKFold 5 folds, whole UTM54S blocks assigned to held fold, 5/10/20km grid sensitivity; no same exact station shared in train/test, no spatial exclusion buffer. Forward-year expanding train outcome years strictly before test year (2004–2014), same station may recur, minimum100 train/10 test pairs,4–8 eligible taxa. Matched rows and model-specific score comparisons. Main Brier/logloss averages first repeated observations within taxon-station, then stations, then taxa, not prevalence-weighted pooled score. ## Interpretation
A continuous historical grass footprint is not equivalent to taxon-specific opportunity through time. A useful next hypothesis is that animal-mediated transport can reach occupied sites yet fail to connect conspecific populations because recipient habitats are compositionally dynamic. The present analysis shows that re-observation of any grass and of prior taxa differ; it does not test animal seed transfer, actual suitability, cause of turnover, colonization of novel sites, or persistence of plants. Missing monthly metadata and detection remain central. No multiplication of these percentages by animal endpoint or germination probabilities; different observations and unknown dependence. No new study count or causal result. No main/Paper A/TNC changes.
