# Recorded life-history trajectories and expansion beyond Torres/Tampa (2026-11-28)
## Design
On the exact archived Torres records used before, compare three observed dates with equal 1-year (A) or 2-year (A/B) spacing. Prior-present taxa partition into 100,101,110,111. Endpoint re-observation = uninterrupted observation + re-observation after intermediate non-recording; not individual survival, actual colonization, demographic turnover or a new mechanism. Paired contrast in initially co-occurring P and nonP taxa, equal coordinate weighting, cluster bootstrap. No interpolation or changed species identities. This is response-open, not new independent validation.
## Independent source expansion
- Moreton Bay Eastern Banks photo-transects, Roelfsema et al. 2015 DOI 10.1038/sdata.2015.40; June 2011 and June 2012 (PANGAEA 846143 and 846146) fixed before acquisition, same month/analyst, species and epiphyte/dark categories, photos ~1m2, non-permanent photo points with GPS error 5–10m. Later use common spatial support, not individual reidentification. Six named taxa, no P strategy represented in the metadata. Independent region and observation method, not an exact P/nonP replication.
- MarineGEO monitoring DOI 10.15468/mt7ggd, IPT v1.2, 492 event rows per metadata, 2976 measurement rows and 2534 occurrence rows; cover/density/epifauna along fixed transects, potentially link vegetation structure to animal community. Metadata is not evidence all rows form repeated surveys or matched animal observations. Preflight actual bytes and measurement semantics only; no result from availability claim.
