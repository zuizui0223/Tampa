# Zostera marina: rarefied offspring allelic richness by vertical position and flowering order (2026-11-25)

## Prior work and question
Follett, Hays & Nepf (2019), DOI 10.3389/fmars.2019.00008, combined a hydrodynamic particle model with seven matched maternal shoots (125 complete seed genotypes), finding higher seed allelic richness in upper than lower spadices (2.87 vs 2.60, original one-tailed paired p=.013), and a greater estimated minimum number of fathers (3.52 vs 2.93 per ten seeds, p=.052). The transport model already explains why mixing and capture depend on canopy-relative release height and surface boundary conditions. Individual-level field data available from authors upon request, not downloaded here. This is prior art, not a new hypothesis of vertical hydrodynamics.

Sgambelluri, Jarvis & Kamel (2024), DOI 10.1002/ece3.11608, explicitly use rhipidium position as a height proxy and spathe position as reproductive-timing proxy; basal indices start at 1. Their original results found no significant rhipidium effect on viability, outcrossing, number of sires or skew, but declining viability and outcrossing with spathe rank. We test a distinct outcome on this second independent field dataset: directly observed offspring allele richness standardized for sample size, without reconstructed parental calls or father labels. This is a posthoc partial replication of the Follett direction, not a reproduction of their exact statistic or new demonstration of flow physics.

## Actual source and computation
Dryad 10.5061/dryad.dfn2z358x, verified original source archive 14912352573 SHA256 c0f4a1b0b988dc4a9f678f10cd8c413b8636585c554fd81591502b4af69d7d61; Seed_Genotypes.xlsx SHA256 394758533a8ef6d26c85c590bba398480f12231c2836294c6dd972c830867b48. 844 unique site/mother/rhipidium/spathe/offspring records, 26 maternal shoots at 3 meadows, 83 branches,162 spathes; 674 fully typed at all ten loci. Both allele calls missing together when locus missing. Used source README and methods to interpret ranks. All mother shoots start at rhipidium 1 among observed records.

For locus with n sampled diploid seeds, allele a is present in c_a seeds; expected richness among k whole seeds sampled without replacement is sum_a [1 - choose(n-c_a,k)/choose(n,k)]. This avoids independent sampling of two alleles from the same seed and retains observed offspring pairs. Arithmetic checks on a three-seed toy case pass. Primary complete-seed panel k=3 chosen for usable matched coverage; additional k=2..6 and locuswise rarefaction with all ten markers having sufficient coverage. No fitted father identities. Follett used eight loci and rarefaction to 12 alleles, so absolute estimates are not interchangeable.

Compare lowest and highest observed rhipidia within mother at identical spathe rank; average each mother's matched spathe differences, then equal mothers. Separate comparison of first and last observed spathe within the same rhipidium (not necessarily actual first/last flowers if unobserved ranks). Hold branch rank or spathe rank constant as appropriate, but no assumption of exact synchrony or physical height. Twenty thousand maternal-shoot bootstrap resamples stratified by observed site, seed 20261125; intervals conditional on three sites and observed selected pairs, not randomized or cross-region intervals. All analysis posthoc, no significance multiplicity claim.

## Flow evidence boundary
Ackerman (1997), https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21708666/, and Ackerman (2002), DOI 10.3732/ajb.89.7.1119, established flow-dependent transport and capture of filamentous pollen. Field velocity attenuation and density effects depend on flow/canopy setting (Lacy & Wyllie-Echeverria 2011 DOI 10.1215/21573698-1152489; Gambi et al. 1990 DOI 10.3354/meps061159; NOAA record https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/27858 for the contrasting flexible-shoot flume result). We did not obtain new raw flow profiles paired to reproductive outcomes; no hydrodynamic coefficient, calibrated dispersal kernel, measured heights, or flowering dates available in the genotype dataset. Do not apply Zostera transport parameters to floating-flower Enhalus. The current contribution is a source-backed comparison of morphological proxies with standardized observed seed richness. Actual mechanism still requires measured canopy-relative flower height, flow, flowering stage, and compatible donor availability together.
