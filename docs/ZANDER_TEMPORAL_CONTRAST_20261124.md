Counts per 100 seeds sown, observed stock only. Both final contrasts' conditional intervals cross zero. Late change = (day93-day40) difference between warm and cold; not disappearance or individual mortality rate. Warm treatments reach half their observed maximum 15 days earlier under both salinities. Excluding T1/T7 late change contrasts -5.11 [-9,-2.67] and -11.47 [-21.25,-3.44]; half-peak contrasts -15.5 and -15.67 days. Leave-one-tank-out late change remains negative in both regimes (-5.83 to -2.67 low, -14.56 to -5.89 ambient). Source paper already reported higher temperature accelerated emergence but not a consistent final advantage. The additional analysis quantifies the change in early-to-final contrast with tank-level uncertainty; no newly discovered temperature effect.

Interpretation: the calendar window in which a plant is exposed to seed versus seedling interactions can vary even when terminal stock differences are uncertain. Combined conceptually with Hediste seed burial and germinated-seed consumption studies, this motivates a phenology-overlap test, NOT evidence that warming increases or decreases predation. Need same site/cohort stage-specific encounter/consumption rate with randomized timing of animal access and temperature, and direct seed fate. Pollination likewise requires overlap with animal activity, not just counts of partners. Do not multiply independent studies into a fitted causal network.
