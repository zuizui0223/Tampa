## Fixed-injection accounting only
For 20,000 injections (divisible by all six designs), replicate each tested plot type without interaction; expected total G2=20,000*mean(G2 per m2)/injections per m2. 400 m2 x10 injections/m2: 5 plots, 2000m2, 18,833 scenario plants vs 400 m2 x50: 1 plot,400m2, 7,233. Same observed mean density, different area. Low dose 2.60x per original injection, high dose 1.92x arithmetic per-area G2 density. This is standardized allocation arithmetic, not measured at equal seed budget, and no spatial interference, habitat limitation or marginal area cost tested. Don't call simulated counts actual future recruits. 

## Independent literature and novelty gate
Orth et al. 2003 DOI 10.3354/meps250071 (https://www.int-res.com/articles/meps2003/250/m250p071.pdf): broadcast seed addition, Chesapeake Bay, initial spring seedlings, seed density effect p=.214 (1999, 1000 vs5000 seeds per 4m2, eight sites; low density arms affected by background seed rain and excluded) and p=.958 in 2000 text (Table 3 .959, four sites, four densities). No observed density effect in this setting, not equivalence; no predator manipulation. PDF text reviewed, screenshots returned cache miss; no graphical digitization. Contrasts cannot be interpreted as same experimental treatment as buried injection or compared by p-values alone.
Zhang et al. 2026 DOI 10.1016/j.ecoleng.2026.108000 (https://scholar2.pusan.ac.kr/resources/01a193c4-48b8-7cca-88b0-9d9b4cc3398b): Jindong Bay, Korea, 4/25/100/400 seeds per 0.25m2 and two water-depth sites. Published abstract: high density maximizes emergence; intermediate 100 seeds maximizes establishment/reproduction and potential seed output (~1.5x highest); shallow site no post-emergence establishment. Density stage dependence independently reported, not a novel claim. Water depth confounded with site characteristics, not proof of depth mechanism. Full text access 403, no public raw data located. No reanalysis or variance imputation.
Gräfnings et al. 2023 DOI 10.1111/rec.13851 reported factorial 2/20 seeds per injection x25/100 injection density x2/4 cm depth; seed amounts per m2 50,200,500,2000, no equal-total contrast. Published main effects on seed recruitment; no raw data located this turn. Further supports need to independently manipulate seed count, number of clusters, spacing, predator access. Not proof of predation-driven density effects.

Next decisive test: fixed 200 seeds distributed among 10/40/100 injection locations (20/5/2 seeds per location), common 4m2 area and randomized placement, crossed with predator access and matched cage control; add low vs high total seed amounts to estimate joint response. Record initial retained viable seeds, seed predation, emergence, reproductive output and next-generation recruitment at the assigned plot level. Quantities illustrative experimental design, not new data. No actual link to otters or animal pollination, no systematic meta-analysis. Main/manuscript untouched.
