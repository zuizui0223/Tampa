## Interpretation
Initial recruitment response can be less than proportional to seed input, and the relative advantage can attenuate by a later observation. Need equal total seed inputs distributed across different numbers/sizes of injection clusters, crossed with predator access and flow, and direct seed-fate and next-year observation. Prior literature explicitly reports density-efficiency tradeoffs; novelty here is harmonized endpoint timing and audit, not discovery of density dependence or a plant–animal feedback. Biological mechanism remains unresolved (clonal spread, competition, weather, sediment and monitoring effects may all contribute). No main or manuscript changes.
