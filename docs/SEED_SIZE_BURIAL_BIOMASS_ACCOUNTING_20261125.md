# From seed size to observed seedling biomass under burial (2026-11-25)

## Independent experiment and scope
Jørgensen, Labouriau & Olesen (2019), PLOS ONE, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0215157. Previously acquired source artifact 14918042341, verified SHA256 74f0c656295996b258907469cbbfd15a75df41744279d39a74947fbd74ad5374. All three member hashes pinned in analysis/184. This is independent of the Sgambelluri seed-size/parentage cohort but not newly acquired or an independent replication of prior analyses of this burial experiment. Original paper already establishes heavier seeds produce larger seedlings, while the emergence response to size is not clear. New descriptive estimand: measured seedling dry-mass sum per nominal planting pot, accounting for number of fully measured seedlings, and an illustrative equal-initial-seed-mass budget comparison. No new claim of individual seed-reserve causation.

Five tanks, 30 sorted-seed pots (one large and one small at each of 2,4,6 cm per tank), 15 planted seeds per pot, ten-week harvest. Unsorted cohorts excluded. Seed mass metric sample, not individually matched to planted seed fates: large n29 mean 4.810137931 mg DW, small n28 mean 2.879571429 mg DW, ratio 1.670435358. Confirmed from separate Seed weight sheet. Paper used visual sorting, so size class is not an isolated manipulation of carbohydrates.

## Hypothetical equal seed dry-mass input
For fixed seed count, observed total seedling mass ratios large/small 1.688472,1.116686,2.373836 at 2/4/6 cm. Dividing by the separately sampled mean initial dry mass ratio 1.670435 gives 1.010797,.668500,1.421088. Complete paired sensitivity 1.072551,.678381,1.465020. This is a hypothetical linear seed-number scaling, not an experimental equal biomass sowing comparison, maternal reproductive cost, efficiency of reserve conversion, or restoration optimum. Density-dependent competition, actual seed production cost, surviving seed bank and later growth could alter the result. Observed viable unemerged seed counts are also different (33 vs 15 at 4 cm); do not count these as established seedlings or convert their number into future biomass. Proposed next experiment: seed size x sowing density x burial, with equal-count and equal-initial-mass arms and actual survival over time.

## Independent physiological literature and acquisition limit
Zhu et al. 2023, DOI 10.3389/fpls.2023.1130292, measured stage-specific physiological samples and reports starch and sucrose contents increasing 31% and 40% respectively between immature and mature seed stages. Sampling is destructive, no individual reserve-to-establishment trajectory; no calibration of the current seed dry mass against carbohydrates. Xu et al. 2016, DOI 10.7717/peerj.2697, uses seed color and moisture content as maturity proxies, and reports raw germination/growth data supplement DOI 10.7717/peerj.2697/supp-1 (Raw_data.xlsx). Publisher article HTTP 429, PMC access challenge, local DNS failures: raw supplement not obtained in this turn. No new physiological fit or direct maturity correction of 2025 seed masses. 2025 five-population seed-trait study DOI 10.1016/j.gecco.2025.e03762 lists data available on request; no raw acquisition. Keep these independent literature observations distinct from executed 2019 original-source calculation.
