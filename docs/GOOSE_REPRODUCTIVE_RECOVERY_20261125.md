## Design and data audit
Nine blocks (2–10), four randomly assigned treatments per block, one plot/arm, 36 plots. 0X undisturbed, 1X Feb and 2X Feb/March in both 2017 and 2018; 4X Jan–Apr 2017 only, no clipping in 2018 (recovery arm, not fourth dose of same schedule). All waterfowl excluded seasonally; artificial clipping, not real grazer manipulation. Original paper methods: central 20x20 cm quadrat for shoot counts, 0.6x0.6 m 36-point grid for genotyping. R code figure y-label says 0.36 m2; README calls date columns vegetative shoots whereas paper Fig2 and methods use total shoots for flowering proportion. Preserve recorded shoot counts S and F; avoid area conversion; primary F/S follows source R code, F/(S+F) sensitivity if S refers only to vegetative. Flower counts are standing stocks at 3 monthly visits, not cumulative unique flowers. March excluded in source. One missing April 2018 S in block9 0X; complete 8 blocks Apr–Jun, all9 May–Jun. No zero imputation.

2478 MLG rows, 72 plot-times, all sample counts match author clone summary, but 2 richness discrepancies: T1_5D G=4 by MLG vs5 summary, T5_9A G=1 vs2. Keep original MLG labels; no corrections. All plot-time coordinate keys unique. Direct set differences recover reported totals 36 gains,41 losses. These are newly detected and no-longer-detected MLGs, not verified sexual births/deaths. End-point sample completeness varies, no new assignment from microsatellite alleles. Comparison excludes discrepant blocks5/9 as sensitivity.

## Genotype detection sensitivity
Raw all9 0X vs2X gain 11 vs1, exact within-block label swap p=.03125 (conditional sharp null; exploratory, unadjusted), loss 4 vs8 p=.3125. 0X vs1X gain 11 vs12, loss4 vs15; 4X gain12 loss14. Same grid positions sampled at both endpoints: mean retained positions 32.33 and33.78 for 0X and2X, gain again 11 vs1. Restriction does not imply sampling all local genets. Rarefaction exact expected detected gain: for each MLG in union of a plot's observed initial and final sets, P(not sampled at T1) * P(sampled at T5), using independent uniform without-replacement samples of size k, conditional on observed labels and counts. k=28 is common minimum of 72 observed samples, also k20/25. Expected gain 0X vs2X k20 .9905/.1859; k25 1.0902/.1575; k28 1.1429/.1421 (difference -1.0008; exact swap p=.02344). Expected standardized detection under a deliberately simplified sample scheme, NOT corrected true immigration. Spatial clustering violates uniform independence as ecological sampling model; matched-grid sensitivity addresses different concern. Excluding blocks5/9 raw difference -.857, p=.125, hence uncertainty about inference with few blocks. Baseline study-wide labels classify 6 observed gains as also seen elsewhere at T1; paper reports at least7 vegetative/rafted events, classification not identical; not equate sampled-baseline absence to sexual origin. 8 plots had no recorded flowering April–June but gain>0; 6 had positive recorded flowering but no gain. Source already discusses vegetative spread, local seed bank, and other mechanisms. No parentage or seed-cohort observation.
