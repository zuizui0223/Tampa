## Research implication
The exact mean decomposition Y=P(Y>0)*E(Y|Y>0) distinguishes arithmetic incidence and magnitude contributions. In saltmarsh predator protection, observed zero avoidance was large; in Campanula singleton source-group contrast positive production dominates. Neither is universal. A genuine comparative synthesis requires independent studies, predeclared endpoint unit and comparable assigned treatment contrasts, complete zero/positive denominators, independent populations/sites and uncertainty estimates; avoid pooling descriptive contrasts. Separate recruitment, flowering, fruit set, seed production, germination, and full-life population growth. Next prioritize direct marine pollination data on individual flower fruit set or pollen tubes, and plant-level full reproductive outcomes, before claiming transfer of terrestrial evidence to marine systems.
