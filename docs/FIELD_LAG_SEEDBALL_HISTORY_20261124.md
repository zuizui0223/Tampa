## Important recording audit
README states integrity 0 means not applicable/not recorded. Naked and buried fireclay all zeros. Among 5 surface formulations, post-baseline 1280 observations: 527 visible-shoot observations all have positive integrity scores, 753 without visible shoots only 1 positive (752 missing/not applicable). Baseline: 134/320 positive scores, all 134 later ever-visible; remaining 186 include 12 ever-visible, 174 never-visible. This documents extreme outcome-dependent availability of recorded integrity; exact reason/protocol unknown. Do not interpret zero as complete disintegration or extrapolate measured scores to all assigned seeds. Source author's R script excludes zeros. Integrity comparisons among observed scores are conditional; no valid prediction or causal mediation of emergence by integrity, no demonstrated predation protection. This does not invalidate the visible-growth counts. Do not use the integrity coverage itself as a predictive feature, including baseline, because of possible response-linked recording.
