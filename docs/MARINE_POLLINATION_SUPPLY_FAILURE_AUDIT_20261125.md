## Thalassia testudinum, van Tussenbroek et al. 2010
Primary source DOI 10.3354/meps08870, https://www.int-res.com/articles/meps2010/419/m419p283.pdf, abstract, Methods/Results. PDF text inspected; screenshot access failed. Do not digitize unobserved figure values.
Flower/bud counts from 10 haphazard 4 m2 quadrats each year; shoot density separately reported site mean. Year 2008: 700 shoots/m2; male 15.0 and female 10.2 fresh flowers or pre-anthesis buds per 4 m2; pollen:ovule 67000; natural control fruit set .86. Year 2009: 689; 8.2; 11.7; 37000; .65. Percent changes -1.5714, -45.3333, +14.7059, -44.7761; fruit set -21 pp. Ratio of site means, male flowers/buds per 1000 shoots: 5.3571 -> 2.9753; not fraction of individually sampled male flowering ramets. The source already reports pollen limitation. Contrast is descriptive across two years, not a longitudinal population model or control for year-level stress.

Within-year higher-supply versus lower-supply contrasts, rounded abstract percentages:
- 2008 unmanipulated control .86 vs 50% male-flower removal .60: +26 pp, fruit ratio 1.4333, nonfruit ratio .35 (65% lower relative nonfruit).
- 2009 artificial supplementation .91 vs natural control .65: +26 pp, fruit ratio 1.4, nonfruit ratio .257143 (74.29% lower relative nonfruit).
Both comparisons have small plot-level replication (two removal and two control plots in 2008; two supplement experiments with controls within same plot in 2009); no flower-level independent inference or pooled causal effect. Distinct treatments and years, do not treat as independent studies. June fruit assessment and July ripe fruit sampling distinct; substantial herbivory and dehiscence between assessments. The reported conditional seed:ovule measurements on remaining fruits cannot establish unconditional seed production per originally marked flower; no product-of-means or incidence/magnitude attribution. No animal manipulation in either field contrast.

## Thalassia animal pollination, van Tussenbroek et al. 2016
Primary source DOI 10.1038/ncomms12980, https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms12980, https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms12980.pdf (also repository mirrors from Edinburgh and VLIZ). Authors established animal-mediated transfer and pollen tube formation in still water. Original first setup with fauna 6 trials, controls 4, 15 minutes: mean added pollen 9 (SEM 3.9), removed 3 (SEM .8), controls no grains added or removed. New arithmetic net mean +6 grains. Unknown within-trial gain/loss covariance means net mean SEM bounded 3.1 to 4.7 from Cauchy-Schwarz, conditional on published rounded SEM. Conventional df=5 t sensitivity gives narrowest illustrative 95% interval [-1.969,13.969] and widest [-6.082,18.082]. Not an actual recovered CI or valid new significance test, and does not negate original evidence of animal-mediated pollen transfer and the separate pollen-tube assay. No count of individual grains that were viable, no mature fruit or seed outcomes. Separate 100-L mesocosm experiment, animal density ~30–90 per L, six distances 15,30,45,60,90,150 cm, five trials displayed; treatment assigned to tank, not flower, and blooms may not open. Fig 6 categories zero, 1–9, 10–99, high (>=100 in figure legend; text wording >100), and no record. Exact counts in abundant samples were not obtained; published chi-square 38.75 df4 cannot uniquely recover cell observations. ## Next empirical gate

Separate flower recruitment, any tube, positive tube load, matured fruit and filled seeds; paired-cohort and time identity required. The current marine experiment establishes a transport mechanism but cannot answer the previous terrestrial comparison's question of failure incidence versus successful-flower output. Main novel direction to test is whether vegetation/animal community change alters *both* availability of reproductive donors and realized transfer capacity, conditional on water movement.
