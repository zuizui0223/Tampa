## Reverse-direction concern
Gräfnings et al. 2024 DOI 10.3389/fmars.2023.1294845 found that H. diversicolor was characteristic of the restored Griend meadow and contributed to the higher macrofauna biomass relative to a nearby bare area. Natural meadow comparison is paired observational (15 sites); restoration site is one area, no randomized manipulation of worms. Authors report raw data available on request, not acquired. Habitat formation can change animal assemblages; contemporaneous co-occurrence cannot identify animal-to-plant causality. Test lagged plant and animal transitions with measured covariates and explicit monitoring coverage rather than interpreting a negative cross-sectional coefficient as a predator effect.
