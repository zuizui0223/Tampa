## Forage (USGS DOI 10.5066/P9T6UB5G)
27,594 records, 11 identical rows. Original author code https://github.com/clint-leach/foraging (process_data.r and bout_level_nb.stan) already models prey counts with number of dives as exposure. Retain domain exclusions as in source code, exclude non-foraging and carryover dives, retain unsuccessful retrievals as zero. Aggregate by bout_id and dive_num first so time is counted once. Exclude dives with missing successful prey counts or incomplete/conflicting time fields. 19,613 domain dives, 17,128 complete eligible cycles, 2,457 bouts; 2,485 excluded. Dive and surface time are seconds in metadata. Use sum of retrieved individuals / sum(dive + surface seconds) times 3600. Surface time may include grooming and other activity; this is a recorded-cycle rate, not actual continuous feeding time. Size class 1 <= one paw width; 2–4 > one paw width; unknown kept separately. These are retrieval counts, not eaten biomass, individual prey survival, population removal, or calories.

13 same named regions with >=5 bouts in each 1993–2007 and 2018–19 window. Equal weight sites, each site has ratio of sums. Small-item rate 30.666 to 293.439 per cycle hour, difference +262.773 [183.246,356.731]; large-item rate 36.561 to 4.731, difference -31.830 [-36.361,-27.719]; total 68.210 to 298.495, difference +230.285 [151.849,321.315]. Small rate increases and large rate decreases in 13/13. Two-sided exact sign p=0.000244 under independent symmetric site signs; this assumption is not established, so treat as descriptive. Intervals are percentile bootstraps of matched site differences, 20,000 draws, seed 20261124, without within-bout, temporal or spatial dependence correction. Retain-duplicate sensitivity same direction; >=10 bouts per window 11/11; June–August restriction 8/8. Different periods may have different sampling effort, observation quality and prey-size recording. No standardized absolute millimetre length and no direct local colonization or density analysis.

## Benthos (USGS DOI 10.5066/P9LODH0Z)
43,900 rows and 1,828 unique observed year/region/zone/site/quadrat combinations from compact original file. Include explicitly empty quadrats in denominator, never infer unobserved quadrats as zero. One EMPTY indicator row has count 1, excluded from organism count. No missing size in positive records. 18 identical rows retained in primary (possible repeated counts), deletion sensitivity gives same matched summary. Clam category only, excludes horse mussels (Modiolus) and urchins. Site-years aggregated per observed quadrat, then equal weight years within period/site, paired sites within zone. Early 1998–2007 versus late 2010–11. Intertidal n=44: all clam counts/quadrat 34.864 to 33.858, diff -1.006 [-7.612,4.252]; >=50mm 3.334 to 1.951, diff -1.383 [-2.461,-.383]. Subtidal n=11: total 55.458 to 30.965, diff -24.494 [-36.587,-14.021]; >=50mm 16.558 to 8.551, diff -8.007 [-14.038,-2.192]. At 40 and 60 mm cutoffs directions persist; intertidal 60-mm interval includes zero. 50 mm is an exploratory threshold, not a trait-defined class. No co-located causal comparison with forage or otter abundance. Prey sampling ends 2011; forage ends 2019. The site metadata and zero-populated table were not acquired; mixed targeted/random sites cannot yet be separated. Need exact site coordinates, sampling design and locally measured structure before any functional interpretation.

## Inference and next data
Leach et al. 2024 (10.1002/ecs2.70084) already documented prey switching, and Leach et al. 2023 (10.1111/1365-2656.13929) modeled butter clam depletion. Here observed time-normalized retrieval rates support a shift in absolute small vs large item encounter, not merely a share change. A different sampling window in benthos suggests loss of large clams can occur while total clam counts remain similar in intertidal habitats. No new causal pathway to seagrass, no measured shell structure or trapping role, no proof of functional loss or persistence. The two size metrics are not equivalent. The next gate is the original forage taxonomy lookup (downloaded by runner but UTF-8 decoding failed before saving bytes), then exact taxon and site crosswalk plus estimates of otter abundance. Preserve raw bytes, verify source MD5 and record encoding rather than silently assign functional groups. Benthic compact files acquired; site metadata, zero-populated table and benthic XML were not. No new Foster, Gross or Jeppesen raw data. Main branch and manuscript unchanged.
