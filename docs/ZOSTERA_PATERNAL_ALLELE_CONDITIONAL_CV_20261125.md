## Conditional prediction
For each site/locus, pooled pollen allele frequency is fitted by penalized conditional multinomial likelihood, accounting for different sets of detectable nonmaternal alleles among mothers. For a mother with genotype alleles M, observed unique paternal allele a has conditional probability p_a / sum_{b not in M} p_b under the pooled model, assuming ordinary diploid inheritance and exchangeable paternal gametes at that locus before conditioning. Add .5 symmetric pseudo-observation per allele, optimize logit likelihood. Vocabulary fixed to the observed locus alphabet including held-out allele names, so not a prospective unknown-allele forecast; no use of held-out frequency counts for fitting. Maternal-specific estimates shrink training mother counts toward the pooled conditional probability with fixed strength 5,20,50 (exploratory sensitivities, no tuning). For each site/locus hold an entire rhipidium out, predict nonmaternal alleles from all other branches; require informative training observations in target mother and at least two mothers in site/locus. Report average negative log probability and equal-mother sensitivity. All candidate predictors conditional on maternal genotypes reconstructed using offspring including held-out branches: not independent validation, no significance tests, no inference of father distances or flowering time.

## Spatial sources and prior-art boundary
No downloadable source with paired mapped father and mother coordinates or observed flowering dates was acquired this turn. Zipperle et al. 2011 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3002472/) estimated median Z. noltii pollen dispersal 1.84–3.22 m under varying candidate father sampling fractions, with assignment incomplete. Sinclair et al. 2014 DOI 10.1093/aob/mcu048 reported mean Posidonia australis pollen dispersal 30.8/26.8 m and clonal size 12.8/13.8 m; cannot pool them as a common dispersal kernel. Furman et al. 2015 DOI 10.1002/lno.10043 report Z. marina pollen distances .57–73.91 m, primary mapped parentage source not obtained. Reusch 2001 DOI 10.1046/j.1420-9101.2001.00257.x already tested patch isolation: patches at least 4 m from other vegetation had no detectable decrease in outcrossing relative to meadow interiors; original discussion proposes higher water exchange across bare patches. Thus the effect of fragment geometry on mating is not universally negative and the prior simulated hard cutoffs are not calibrated. Need paired measured coordinates, flowering dates, independent adult genotype calls and actual pollen/seed outcomes to distinguish spatial, temporal and reconstruction effects. No new spatial result, independent dataset validation or mechanism. Draft exploratory reproductive-interaction branch only; Paper A/TNC and main unchanged.
