## Independent two-generation trial
Gräfnings 2023 DOI 10.1111/1365-2664.14405, data DOI 10.34894/FKSY2M, file hash 8677af34cdc98b0ccde8b9e257a9acd9a670389f84bcd65c82d36146b19d0643. 144 density observations, 4 subsamples per plot-year, 18 plots (3 scale x2 injection density x3 blocks), 90 August 2020 plants (5 per plot); unique joins with elevation and drone-emergence tables. All plot G1 and G2 >0. Densities are plants/m2, seeding density is injections/m2, not individual seeds. July 2020 G1 and August 2021 G2. Ratio G2/G1 is observed stock change between generations, not individual fecundity/survival or exact seed-to-adult probability. Arithmetic plot-equal means for 4/40/400 m2 at 10 injections: .536/.989/1.510; at 50: .280/.340/.712. 400 m2 greater than either small scale for all six block-density pairs. Six pairs within 3 blocks not independent 6 sites. Prior paper already reported scale effects; additional result is generation-ratio accounting.

Identity G2/G1 = spathes per G1 plant * G2/(G1*spathes per plant); spathes measured August on sampled plants while G1 July density. Product is reproductive proxy, not measured seed production, dispersal, or causal mediator. Within matched block/density, geometric mean of 400/4 ratio 3.450 = 1.938 spathes factor x1.780 proxy-conversion factor; 400/40 1.789 =2.014 x.888. Thus scale-associated advantage vs 40 m2 aligns with reproduction proxy, not higher conversion per proxy. Three blocks only, no inference that one mechanism causally dominates; conversion includes timing, pollen, seed viability, retention, establishment, etc. No fauna measured. Internal leave-one-whole-block-out ridge (alpha 1; .1 and 10 sensitivities), target log G2: G1 1.01053 RMSE; G1+scale .89784; G1+spathes .95915; G1+July percentage centre emerged above low-tide water .79987; G1+spathes+dry .86191. Incremental MSE improvement for dry over G1 37.35%. At alpha .1/10 dry RMSE .80225/.88821 vs G1 1.00947/1.04664. Only 3 blocks, response-open comparisons, no causal mediation or independent-site validation. Original study already linked plot scale to desiccation, reproduction and recruitment. Do not claim a new physical mechanism or attribute to worms.
